Climbix

Privacy policy

Updated November 11, 2026.

Climbix (climbix.app) is a football game that is free to play. This page explains what data the game stores, why, and how to ask for it to be deleted.

Without an account

Your game is saved only on your device, in the browser's storage: careers, profile, coins and settings. Clearing your browser data deletes all of it.

If you send us your feedback through the game, we store the answers you chose to give, the same random device code (only to prevent repeat submissions), the game version and the browser type. No name, email or account. Do not write personal data in the free-text field.

To know whether people come back to play, the game sends an anonymous count: a first visit, a return the next day or the next week, a session, and that a career started, moved to a new season or ended. The server only adds up how many times each thing happened each day. No device code, account, IP or browser goes with it.

If the game hits an error, it sends the error message, the game version, the screen you were on and the browser type, so we can fix it. No name, account or device code.

With an account

When you create an account to save to the cloud, we store:

  • Username, the name of your account.
  • Password, if you create one. It is stored irreversibly (hashed): not even we can see the password.
  • Your game: careers, profile, purchased teams and your coin balance, so you can continue on another device.
  • Sessions for each connected device: an access code (stored as a hash), when you signed in, when it was last used, and whether you signed in with a password or through a service.

Coin purchases

When you buy coins, the payment takes place on the Mercado Pago page. Payment details (card, tax ID, e-mail, account) stay with Mercado Pago and follow its privacy policy: we do not receive or store any of them.

What we send to Mercado Pago is only the pack, the price and a random purchase code, without your username. On our server we keep, linked to your account, a record of each purchase: the pack, the number of coins, the amount, the dates, the status and the payment number at Mercado Pago. It is used to deliver the coins, avoid delivering them twice and handle refund requests, and may be kept for as long as the law requires.

Signing in with Google, GitHub or Discord

If you use one of these services to sign in, we receive from it only:

  • your account's identifier on the service, to recognize you next time;
  • a display name for the "Sign-in methods" screen: your email on Google, your @username on GitHub or your name on Discord;
  • on your first sign-in, your name on the service, used only to suggest a username and deleted right after.

We ask only for basic identification permissions (Google: email and profile; GitHub: public profile data; Discord: identify). We do not receive your password for these services, we do not access contacts, files or messages, we do not post anything on your behalf, and we do not keep the access the service gives us: it is used once, at sign-in, and discarded. You can disconnect a service at any time in Account → Sign-in methods.

Security

To block mass attempts to guess passwords, the server counts failed attempts per username and per IP address. The site's hosting provider (Hostinger) keeps technical access logs, like any website.

Leaderboard

The leaderboard is turned off in this version of the game. Careers sent before remain stored under the nickname you chose and a random device code, and can be deleted on request.

What we don't do

We do not sell or share your data, we do not show ads, and we do not use cookies or tracking tools. The data is used only to make the game work: saving, signing in to your account and delivering purchased coins.

Your rights

Under the LGPD (Brazil's data protection law), you can ask to see, correct or delete your data. To delete your account, your cloud save, your coins, your connected services and old leaderboard careers, write to contato@climbix.app with your username. Signing out in the game stops saving to the cloud, but does not delete your account.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top of the page is updated. See also the Terms of use.
