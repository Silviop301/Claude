Climbix

Privacy policy

Updated October 2, 2026.

Climbix (climbix.app) is a free football career game. This page explains what data the game stores, why, and how to ask for it to be deleted.

Without an account

Your game is saved only on your device, in the browser's storage: careers, collection, Hall of Fame, achievements, Trophy Room and settings. Clearing your browser data deletes all of it.

For the leaderboard, the game sends our server a random device code and a summary of each career (the name of the player created in the game, position, country, club, rating, goals, assists, titles, awards and seasons). A career only appears on the leaderboard under the nickname you choose.

If you send us your feedback through the game, we store the answers you chose to give, the same random device code (only to prevent repeat submissions), the game version, the position, seasons and rating of the career that just ended, and the browser type. No name, email or account. Do not write personal data in the free-text field.

With an account

When you create an account to save to the cloud, we store:

  • Username, which also becomes your name on the leaderboard.
  • Password, if you create one. It is stored irreversibly (hashed): not even we can see the password.
  • Your game: career in progress, collection, Hall of Fame, achievements, daily career and Trophy Room, so you can continue on another device.
  • Sessions for each connected device: an access code (stored as a hash), when you signed in, when it was last used, and whether you signed in with a password or through a service.

Signing in with Google, GitHub or Discord

If you use one of these services to sign in, we receive from it only:

  • your account's identifier on the service, to recognize you next time;
  • a display name for the "Sign-in methods" screen: your email on Google, your @username on GitHub or your name on Discord;
  • on your first sign-in, your name on the service, used only to suggest a username and deleted right after.

We ask only for basic identification permissions (Google: email and profile; GitHub: public profile data; Discord: identify). We do not receive your password for these services, we do not access contacts, files or messages, we do not post anything on your behalf, and we do not keep the access the service gives us: it is used once, at sign-in, and discarded. You can disconnect a service at any time in Account → Sign-in methods.

Security

To block mass attempts to guess passwords, the server counts failed attempts per username and per IP address. The site's hosting provider (Hostinger) keeps technical access logs, like any website. The game's 3D library is loaded from jsDelivr, which receives your IP address when delivering the file.

What we don't do

We do not sell or share your data, we do not show ads, and we do not use cookies or tracking tools. The data is used only to make the game work: saving, signing in to your account and building the leaderboard.

Your rights

Under the LGPD (Brazil's data protection law), you can ask to see, correct or delete your data. To delete your account, your cloud save, your connected services and your careers on the leaderboard, write to silviop301@gmail.com with your username. Signing out in the game stops saving to the cloud, but does not delete your account.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top of the page is updated. See also the Terms of use.
