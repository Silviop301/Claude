The game

Climbix is a football game that is free to play. The careers, matches and news are fictional. Names of clubs, national teams, competitions and people appear only as references and do not indicate any official connection with them.

Coins and purchases

  • Coins are an in-game item. You earn coins by playing and, if you wish, you can buy packs in the Shop. Buying is optional: the whole game can be played without paying.
  • They are used to choose the club your career starts at and to add funds to the club you manage. No purchase is a draw: the pack shows how many coins it holds and what it costs before you pay.
  • You need an account to buy, because coins are stored in it. They have no cash value, cannot be exchanged for money or moved to another account.
  • Payment is handled by Mercado Pago, in Brazilian reais, with the methods it offers (Pix and card, for example). Coins are added to your account when Mercado Pago confirms the payment. The charge appears under the name CLIMBIX.
  • If you are under 18, buy only with permission from a parent or guardian.

Refunds

  • You can cancel a purchase within 7 days, as long as the coins from that pack have not been used. We return the full amount through the same payment method and the coins leave your account.
  • If you paid and the coins did not arrive, or you were charged twice, we deliver the coins or return the amount, whichever you prefer.
  • To ask, write to contato@climbix.app with your username and the date of the purchase.
  • Coins already used and purchases older than 7 days are not refunded, except where the law guarantees it.
